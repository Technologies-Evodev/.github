Our bilingual (EN/FR) operations platform puts projects, clients, sites, teams, and inspections in one app:

- **Core modules:** Employees, Organisations, Contacts, Sites / buildings, Projects, Inspections
- **Optional modules:** Time sheets, Quotes
- **Customization:** Custom fields, forms, and item types; a template builder; diagrams; PDF report layouts

## 🤝 How We Work

1. **Scope before code.** We don't start building until we understand the problem well enough to give you a real timeline and a real price.
2. **Show working software early.** You'll see and use real progress throughout the project, not just at the end.
3. **Build for handoff.** Documented, maintainable code, whether we support it long-term or your internal team takes it over.
